About these estimates

Route distance uses the nearest point on the GPX; crossings can be ambiguous. The gold line shows that match. Route average pace is elapsed time ÷ route miles covered. Finish estimates use that pace and the remaining route distance.

GPS distance and GPS pace use recorded movement. Both pace estimates appear after 0.1 mile and hide when reports are stale. Elapsed time includes stops from the saved race start. Mile labels are spaced every two route miles.

Location reports refresh about once a minute while this page is visible. The latest-position marker expires after 15 minutes. Stopping sharing clears recorded run data. This is not an emergency tracking service.
